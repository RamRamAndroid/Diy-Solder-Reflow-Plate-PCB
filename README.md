# Details
70mm x 50mm 

12VDC (5A Minimum)

5.5mm x 2.5mm Barrel Jack

# Why Did I Make This
Solder reflow plates online are too expencive and small so i made a cheaper better version

# How To Build
First download the gerber file in the releases and then get it manufactured at pcb way or jlc pcb and order a 12v 5a power supply and a dc barrel jack and a ksd9700 (below or exactly 260c) then solder the dc jack in its place and then add solder to the 'Fill With Solder' hole then solder the ksd9700 to the holes labelled ksd9700 and wait for it to cool down and mount the ksd9700 behind the hot plate surface with thermal pads or thermal paste and turn it on and wait for 2min and u can solder with it
